My Heart on Paper

A collection of all the things I feel for you.

You are not just my wife, you are my best friend, my soul, my today and all of my tomorrows. Every morning I wake up next to you is a reminder of how incredibly lucky I am.

Thank you for making my life so beautiful just by being in it. Your smile is the only sunshine I ever need, and your love is the anchor that keeps me steady.

I promise to love you in every lifetime, in every world, in every story. If I had a flower for every time I thought of you, I could walk through my garden forever.

They say when you meet the love of your life, time stops. What they didn't tell me is that when it starts again, it moves so fast because every moment with you is precious.

I love you not only for what you are, but for what I am when I am with you. You bring out the absolute best in me, and I want to spend the rest of my life making you happy.

No matter what happens, no matter where we are, I will always be yours. My heart recognized yours the moment we met, and it has been entirely yours ever since.

Forever Yours
